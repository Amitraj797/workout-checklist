Injury Safety Rules ▼
Right Elbow (Healed Fracture)
Use neutral/hammer grip on all curls and presses
No skull crushers, no heavy barbell curls
Wear an elbow sleeve on upper body days
Stop any exercise causing sharp elbow pain - switch to alternate
Left Clavicle (Healed Fracture)
Dumbbells only for chest pressing (never barbell)
Machine shoulder press only - no barbell overhead press
No wide-grip pulldowns or dips for weeks 1-4
If collarbone discomfort on bench - switch to floor press

Intensity Technique Guide
ElementWhat It Means
RIR: 2Reps In Reserve — stop 2 reps before failure
DROPDrop Set — last set, reduce weight 30-40%, immediately do more reps
FAILUREGo to Failure — last set, keep going until you can't with good form
SUPERSETSuperset — do both exercises back-to-back, no rest between them

Progressive Overload (Weeks 1-8) ▼
WeeksWhat to Do
1-2Learn all movements. Light weights (RPE 5-6). Focus 100% on form.
3-4Increase weight by ~2 kg per DB exercise, ~5 kg per machine.
5-6Add 1 set to compound lifts (to 4 sets). Increase planks to 40-45s.
7-8Increase weight again. Reduce rest by 10-15s. Add 2 min to cardio.
Every 4 wksDeload week - drop weights 40%, keep all movements.
